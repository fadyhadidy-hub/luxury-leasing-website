ABOUT US

Excellence in Every Agreement

Prime Power Leasing was founded on a simple belief: leasing should empower, not complicate. We're here to provide premium vehicles and properties with terms that respect your time, your goals, and your lifestyle.

OUR STORY

Who We Are

Prime Power Leasing, LLC was established to fill a gap in the market—a leasing company that treats individuals with the same care and professionalism typically reserved for corporate clients. We recognized that people seeking premium vehicles and real estate deserved better: clearer terms, genuine expertise, and service that goes beyond transactions.

Today, we specialize exclusively in serving individual clients who value quality, flexibility, and trust. Our portfolio includes luxury vehicles, executive sedans, high-performance cars, residential properties, and vacation homes—all available with leasing terms designed around your life, not ours.

We've built our reputation one relationship at a time, earning trust through transparency, delivering on promises, and treating every client like they're our only client.
