## Notes

- Offers are subject to change or early termination by the card issuer.
- Transfer bonuses generally apply to the **bonus miles received**, not a flat rate — verify final amounts on the issuer's transfer portal before initiating.
- Capital One JAL base transfer ratio is 1,000:750 (not 1:1); the 30% bonus brings it to 1,000:975.
- Chase Aeroplan bonus cardholders receive 30% instead of the standard 20% when transferring ≥50,000 UR points in a single transaction.
- Citi ThankYou is also offering bonuses this month (30% Virgin Atlantic, 25% Avianca through Apr 18) — outside scope of this scan but worth noting.
