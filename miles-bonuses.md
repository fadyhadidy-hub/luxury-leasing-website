- **Chase → Marriott 55%**: Applies to Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Preferred, Freedom Unlimited, and Ink cards. Best value when Marriott has category-rate award availability.

- **AMEX → Flying Blue 25%**: Applies to all Membership Rewards-earning cards (Platinum, Gold, Green, Business Gold/Platinum). Flying Blue miles work on Air France, KLM, and SkyTeam partners.
