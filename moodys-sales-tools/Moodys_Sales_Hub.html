Directors & Officers liability covers executives, board members and the entity against claims alleging wrongful acts in managing the organization — securities suits, breach of fiduciary duty, creditor and regulatory actions.

Surety
Credit, not insurance — backing a principal's promise to perform
Moody's
Data Advantage
●●●●●
📌 What It Is
Surety bonds guarantee a principal (contractor / licensee) will fulfil an obligation to an obligee. The surety expects zero losses and relies on indemnity — so it is fundamentally a credit decision.
👥 Who Buys It
  • 🏗️ General contractors
  • 🔧 Specialty subcontractors
  • 🏢 Developers
  • 📋 Licensed professionals (court & license bonds)
  • 🏛️ Public-sector obligees
😰 What Keeps the Underwriter Up at Night
  • 🔹 Working capital & tangible net worth
  • 🔹 Trade-payment behavior (do they pay subs on time?)
  • 🔹 Backlog size & gross-margin fade
  • 🔹 Liens, judgments & UCC filings
  • 🔹 Banking relationship & liquidity
🚩 Deal-Breakers (Red Flags)
  • 🚩 Negative working capital
  • 🚩 Negative gross profit in backlog
  • 🚩 Active bond claim or obligee dispute
  • 🚩 Unsatisfied tax lien or judgment

Insuring the money your client is owed

Data Advantage

📌 What It Is
Trade Credit insurance protects sellers against buyer non-payment from insolvency or protracted default — plus political and currency-transfer risk on export trade.
👥 Who Buys It
  • 🌍 Exporters
  • 🏭 Manufacturers
  • 📦 Distributors & wholesalers
  • 🏦 Banks with trade-finance books
  • 💰 Factoring companies
😰 What Keeps the Underwriter Up at Night
  • 🔹 Buyer-portfolio credit quality
  • 🔹 Country & political risk
  • 🔹 Buyer concentration (single-name risk)
  • 🔹 Payment trends (DSO / Days-Beyond-Terms)
  • 🔹 Policyholder moral hazard
🚩 Deal-Breakers (Red Flags)
  • 🚩 Sanctioned buyer or buyer country
  • 🚩 Buyer insolvency filing
  • 🚩 Single buyer >50% of the portfolio
  • 🚩 Distressed policyholder (adverse selection)
